Tu pitch de 30 segundos

Una conversación corta, clara y sin presión.

No intentes vender todo en la primera llamada. Busca entender si existe un problema real y abre la puerta al diagnóstico.

Guion en inglés

“Hi, I work with businesses that get leads from social media, their website, or calls. We help them respond faster, organize every inquiry, and follow up automatically so fewer opportunities slip through. How are you currently handling new leads?”

Recuerda

01Pregunta antes de explicar.
02Habla de su proceso actual, no de herramientas.
03Escucha una dificultad concreta.
04Propón una llamada para revisar su flujo.

Preguntas que abren ventas

No adivines el problema. Haz preguntas que lo hagan visible.

LEADS

“Where do most of your new inquiries come from: Instagram, Facebook, your website, referrals, or calls?”

VELOCIDAD

“How quickly does someone usually get a reply when they message you?”

SEGUIMIENTO

“What happens when a lead does not book or buy on the first conversation?”

OPERACIÓN

“Who is responsible for keeping track of every new inquiry?”

TIEMPO

“What repetitive questions does your team answer every day?”

OBJETIVO

“If you could improve one part of your lead process this month, what would it be?”

Objeciones frecuentes

Responde con calma. El objetivo es entender, no presionar.

“We already have a CRM.”“That is great. We can look at how your current process connects your conversations, follow-up, and team. Often the opportunity is in making the tools work together.”
“I don't need AI.”“That makes sense. The goal is not to add technology for its own sake; it is to help your team respond and follow up more consistently.”
“It sounds complicated.”“We design it around the way you already work. We start with the most valuable part of the process and keep it practical.”
“How much does it cost?”“It depends on the channels, workflow, and goals. The first step is a short review so we can recommend the right scope instead of selling something you do not need.”
